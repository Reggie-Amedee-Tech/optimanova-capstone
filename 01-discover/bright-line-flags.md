# Bright-line flags from these interviews

Some decisions belong to the OptimaNova Advisor, not to us. These are the six bright lines:

1. **Line 1:** scope, fee, or timeline changes
2. **Line 2:** final governance recommendations and policy sign-off
3. **Line 3:** sensitive data decisions (donor, beneficiary, student, health, or HR data moving anywhere new)
4. **Line 4:** vendor or tool selections and commitments
5. **Line 5:** legal, compliance, or ethics concerns, including anything that merely feels off
6. **Line 6:** public, board-facing, or funder-facing representations

Our Nov 6 interviews surfaced the items below. We flag them here, carry them into the Governance one-pager, and route them to our Advisor, who owns every final call.
