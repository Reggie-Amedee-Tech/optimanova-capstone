Our team is presenting the Saturday Volunteer Planner that Enrique built in Week 3. Discover therefore does two jobs. Part 1 tests whether the problem the Planner solves holds up in independent interviews. Part 2 gives a source for each supporting claim that Diagnose and Design make.

## Part 1: Does the evidence behind the Planner hold up?

On Nov 6, Reginald interviewed Gloria Ortiz (Operations & Finance Manager) and Marcus Bell (Programs Director). Gloria is the Planner's user, and Marcus runs distribution day to day.

| What the Planner is built on | Enrique's source | Reginald's Nov 6 interview | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gloria spends about 4 hours a week scheduling, confirming, and reminding volunteers | Gloria, Nov 2 (Enrique) | Gloria: "about four hours every week" | Corroborated |
| Saturday no-shows: about 5 of the usual 25 | Gloria, Nov 2 (Enrique) | Gloria: about 5 of 25 didn't show on one Saturday last month | Corroborated as **one example**, not a rate |
| Empty volunteer spots delay setup | Not cited in Design | Marcus: empty spots "directly delays" pantry setup, and he said this matches what he sees on the ground | New second voice |
| When about 5 of 25 volunteers don't show, morning prep is set back 30-45 minutes | Design cites only Reginald's Nov 6 interview | Gloria: short-handed Saturdays "can set the morning prep back by thirty to forty-five minutes" | New evidence from this interview |
| Texting runs through Gloria's personal phone, and she named the mixing as a problem | Design cites only Reginald's Nov 6 interview | Gloria: volunteer messages get "mixed into my personal cell phone" | New evidence from this interview |
| Success means a reliable roster and a tool volunteers will actually use | Design cites only Reginald's Nov 6 interview | Gloria: "simple and accessible enough that our volunteers will actually use it" | New evidence from this interview |

### What our interviews add

- **A second voice.** Marcus independently confirmed the impact on the pantry side, which the Planner's baseline didn't have before.
- **The intake workflow.** Marcus walked us through a 25-minute family intake: 8 minutes on a paper form, 6 typing it into a Google Sheet, 7 retyping the same information into the city portal, 3 on a case note, and 1 filing. That is the source for roadmap step 4. It also supports the roadmap order, since the volunteer data is lighter than the family data that intake handles.
- **Scale.** The pantry serves about 450 households a month across Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays (Marcus).
