# Workflow maps

## Workflow 1: Volunteer scheduling (Gloria Ortiz, Nov 6)

| Step | What happens | Who | Minutes | Tool | Data touched |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Volunteers put their names on a sign-up sheet at the pantry entrance | Volunteers | Not asked | Paper sheet | Names |
| 2 | Later in the week, Gloria texts the group to confirm who is coming | Gloria | ~4 hrs/week total (steps 2-4) | Personal phone | Names, confirmation status |
| 3 | Gloria tracks the replies back and forth | Gloria | Included above | Personal phone | Confirmation status |
| 4 | The day before, Gloria texts reminders on where to park and when to arrive | Gloria | Included above | Personal phone | Shift details, contact info |
| 5 | **Exception:** no-shows lead to last-minute texting on Saturday morning, and prep runs late | Gloria | 30-45 min delay when short-handed (Gloria) | Personal phone | Contact info |

## Workflow 2: Benefits intake (Marcus Bell, Nov 6)

| Step | What happens | Who | Minutes | Tool | Data touched |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Family fills out a paper form at the front desk | Family | ~8 | Paper form | Name, address, household size, income, benefits needed |
| 2 | Case manager types the form into the intake sheet | Case manager | ~6 | Google Sheet | Same data |
| 3 | Case manager **retypes the same information** into the city portal | Case manager | ~7 | City benefits portal | Same data |
| 4 | Case manager writes a quick case note | Case manager | ~3 | Not stated | Case note |
| 5 | Case manager files the paper form | Case manager | ~1 | Paper file | Paper form |

Total: about 25 minutes per family, for first-time visitors (Marcus). Step 3 is the double entry. Typing into the Sheet and the portal together takes about 13 minutes.

Marcus's success measure: "a family's information only had to be entered once instead of being retyped over and over."
