## 1. Welcome (about 0:30)

> Hi, I'm Reginald, and this is a short walkthrough of the Saturday Volunteer Planner, a helper that drafts your Saturday volunteer plan. Gloria shared that scheduling and reminders take about four hours a week, and last month about five of the usual 25 volunteers didn't show.
>
> One rule: the Planner drafts, and you decide. Nothing goes out until you approve it and send it yourself. Everything today uses made-up names.

## 2. Show (about 2:00)

*Fresh chat. Paste the Show list and submit.*

> I'm giving it first names, shifts, and languages. No phone numbers or addresses.

*Answer any questions it asks (no job targets; this is the complete list). When the plan appears:*

> This is a starting plan, and staff make final placements on Saturday. Here's the coverage gap against our usual 25, and here's the orientation notice, signed from Gloria. Translated versions say "Needs fluent review," so a fluent staff member checks them first.
>
> Specifics check: every name matches the shift they signed up for.

*If a volunteer is listed under a shift they didn't choose, correct it:* `Lena and Sam signed up for the 8:45 AM to 11:00 AM shift only. Please remove them from Shift 2 and show the corrected plan.`

*Then type:* `Maya and Darnell didn't show up. Please revise the plan.`

> On Saturday, I type who's missing. It suggests moves within each person's own shift, and I decide which to approve. It never texts anyone and never calls the plan final.

## 3. Try (about 2:30)

*New chat. A staff member shares their screen and pastes the Try list.*

> **Staff:** Two people are missing details, so let's see what it does.

*The Planner asks for Nia's shift and Owen's language.*

> **Staff:** It asked instead of guessing. Nia chose 11 to 1, and Owen prefers English.

*Staff types:* `Nia: 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Owen: English.`

> **Staff:** I check it with the Four Checks. Source: does every name and shift match my sheet? Specifics: names, shifts, dates, and arrival times. Sense: does it fit how our Saturday works? Sensitivity: nothing private.

*Staff types:* `Marcel didn't show up. Please revise the plan.`

> **Staff:** I approve this move because [reason]. I reject this one because [reason]. Marcus or the floor lead makes the final placements.

The trainer stays quiet during Try.

## 4. Check (about 1:00)

*Show the handoff doc. Staff answer without looking.*

1. "The Planner says a volunteer changed shifts, but you never confirmed it. What do you do before anything is shared?" Expect: reject the change, check the sign-up sheet or ask the volunteer, run the Four Checks, send it yourself.
2. "Who makes the final placements on Saturday?" Expect: Marcus Bell or the floor lead.
3. "If the Gem stops working, what do you do?" Expect: make a new Gem from the saved instructions; if that fails, stop, tell your Advisor, and use the paper sheet.

**Pass:** all three answered correctly. If one is missed, replay that part of Show.

## 5. Close (about 0:30)

> The handoff page has everything you just did. For four weeks, track four numbers: weekly scheduling time, Saturday no-shows, minutes rearranging on Saturday morning, and how late the doors open. Real volunteer names go in only after your OptimaNova Advisor and Linden Street approve. Thanks for watching.
