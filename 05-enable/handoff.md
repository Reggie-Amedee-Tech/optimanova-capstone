# Saturday Volunteer Planner: Handoff for Linden Street Staff

**Owner:** Reginald Amedee | **Backup:** Dwayne Saxton
*Built from Enrique Quezada's Week 3 handoff doc and training outline, refined with Reginald's Nov 6 interviews. Drafted with AI help.*
*Draft for Advisor review. Real volunteer data needs approval from your OptimaNova Advisor and Linden Street first.*

## What it does

You type in your Saturday sign-up list (first name, shift, preferred language). The Planner drafts a starting plan across unloading, packing bags, check-in, and handing food out, lists gaps against the usual 25 volunteers, and drafts an orientation notice signed from you. On Saturday, tell it who didn't show and it suggests same-shift moves for you to approve. The paper sign-up sheet stays as it is, and you still send every message yourself.

## How to run it

1. Open the Planner: https://gemini.google.com/gem/1872iGl0XTnIa5gLzMNIi-7uIuaso8JCD?usp=sharing
2. Enter the Saturday date, then each volunteer's first name, shift, and preferred language from the sign-up sheet.
3. If it asks a question (a missing shift or language, which Saturday, job targets), answer from the sheet or ask the volunteer. Never guess.
4. Read the plan and notice with the **Four Checks**:
   - **Source:** does every name and shift match the sheet?
   - **Specifics:** check names, shifts, dates, and arrival times.
   - **Sense:** does it fit how your Saturday works?
   - **Sensitivity:** no phone numbers, addresses, or private details.
5. Fix anything wrong, copy the notice into your own message, and send it yourself. The Planner can't send anything.
6. Spanish or Haitian Creole text is marked "Needs fluent review." A fluent staff member checks it before it goes out.
7. On Saturday, type who is missing (for example, "Maya and Darnell didn't show up. Please revise the plan."). Approve only the moves that work. Marcus Bell or the floor lead makes the final placements.

Until your Advisor and Linden Street approve real data, use made-up names only.

## What to do when it breaks

- **It adds a wrong or made-up detail:** don't share it. Fix it in your copy and tell the Planner what was wrong.
- **It moves someone to another shift or calls the plan final:** reject it and remind it that volunteers stay in the shift they chose.
- **The Gem or link stops working** (Google starts converting personal-account Gems to skills on Nov 17, 2026): make a new Gem and paste the saved Gem instructions. If Google converts it to a skill, review and test it before relying on it.
- **Still not fixed:** stop using it, tell your OptimaNova Advisor, and plan from the paper sheet.

## What it won't do

Send messages or text volunteers; store the volunteer list; change the real schedule or make final placements; move anyone to a shift they didn't choose; guess missing details; or treat Spanish or Haitian Creole text as final. Version 1 doesn't take texting off your personal phone. That is the next step on the roadmap.
